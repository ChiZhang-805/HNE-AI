\subsection{Directed Prediction-Gain Networks}
CNM-GC averages first-order Granger influence from a dominant group to the remaining indicators, then takes the reciprocal \citep{hneM16}. Our past-only adaptation forms two variance clusters from the available 32-day history. It requires at least 12 jointly observed triples per directed edge and retains the score's original increasing-risk direction. A separate scalar logistic readout learns its orientation on training participants. Table~\ref{tab:directed} uses 1,942 origins from 55 RESILIENT participants and 1,199 origins from 19 BrisT1D participants, where CNM and the original primary methods are all defined.

Two representation comparisons retain HNE's history, environment, reference fit, and additive predictor. They replace correlation summaries by first-order prediction-gain changes over 8, 16, and 32 days. The second subtracts the Gaussian independent-row null expectation, $\psi((n-2)/2)-\psi((n-3)/2)$, from each gain estimated with $n$ triples. On the original primary populations, their AUROCs are 0.797/0.796 for RESILIENT and 0.623/0.626 for BrisT1D, versus 0.802/0.709 for the original representation. The correlation-based representation remains the main HNE model.
